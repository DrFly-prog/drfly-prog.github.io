Delete your Essryn account and data

This page explains how to request deletion of your account and associated data for Essryn: The Mage's Path, developed by DrFly Game Studio.

Option 1 — Delete in the app (instant)

Open Essryn and go to Menu → Settings → Delete account, then confirm. Your account and cloud save are permanently deleted right away.

Option 2 — Request by email

If you can no longer access the app, email drfly.prog@gmail.com from the address linked to your account, with the subject Delete my Essryn account. We will verify your request and delete your data within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your account and email address
  • Your cloud save (essences, cities, talents, territories, progress)
  • Your public profile and leaderboard entry (mage name, level, stats)
  • Your friends list and duel history

What may be retained

We do not keep personal data after deletion. Anonymous, non-identifying diagnostic logs (e.g. crash data) may persist for a limited time and cannot be linked back to you. Advertising identifiers are managed by Google and your device settings, not stored by us.
